In the rapidly advancing world of artificial intelligence, one of the most remarkable innovations in natural language processing is the development of Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT). Introduced by OpenAI, GPT represents a major leap forward in enabling machines to understand and generate human-like text. It has transformed the way we interact with technology, from chatbots and content generation tools to research assistants and educational aids. GPT is based on the transformer architecture, a deep learning model introduced in 2017 that excels in handling sequences, such as text. Unlike previous models that relied heavily on labeled data and task-specific training, GPT follows a pre-training and fine-tuning approach.sup In its first stage, it is trained on a vast corpus of text from books, websites, and articles to learn grammar, facts about the world,sub reasoning abilities, and even some level of emotional intelligence. In the second stage, it is fine-tuned on specific tasks like translation, summarization, or question answering.
